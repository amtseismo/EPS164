## Purpose

In previous chapters, we focused primarily on seismic wave propagation through the Earth. In this chapter, we shift our attention to the **earthquake source itself** and how fault motion generates seismic waves.

In this chapter, we will:

- describe fault slip and earthquake rupture
- develop the double-couple source model
- understand seismic moment and moment magnitude
- examine how earthquakes radiate seismic energy

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lecture, you should be able to:

- describe elastic rebound theory
- explain how fault slip generates seismic waves
- define seismic moment and moment magnitude
- describe the double-couple earthquake source
- identify nodal planes and radiation patterns
- explain the physical meaning of focal mechanisms
- relate rupture properties to observed ground motion
